The frequency response is characterized by a warm, rolled-off high end and a deep, sustained sub-bass foundation. The dynamic aesthetic is smooth and compressed, prioritizing a cohesive "wall of sound" texture over sharp transient attacks, enhancing the dreamlike quality.
Vocal Details
Vocal Gender & Timbre: A single female vocalist delivers the performance with a soft, breathy, and ethereal timbre. Her voice possesses a delicate, airy quality that blends seamlessly into the instrumental texture rather than cutting sharply above it.
Vocal Style: The delivery is consistently intimate and restrained, employing a near-whisper technique that emphasizes vulnerability. The phrasing is fluid and often lags slightly behind the beat, reinforcing the song's theme of flowing. There is a gradual build in vocal layering towards the end, but the core performance remains gentle and unforced throughout.
Harmony/Backing Vocals: Extensive self-harmonies are utilized, particularly in the refrain sections. These layers are stacked to create a choral, swelling effect that surrounds the lead line, adding density and emotional resonance without introducing a distinct second voice.
Vocal FX: The vocals are heavily processed with long-tail reverb and delay, creating a washed-out, distant effect that places the singer deep within the mix. Subtle pitch correction and formant shifting may be present to enhance the otherworldly, synthetic feel of the voice.
