Global Metadata
Basic Attributes: bpm is 94. key is D, and scale is minor. Alternative R&B / Electronic Pop.
Global Emotional Progression: The piece opens with a disoriented, dreamlike atmosphere that gradually grounds itself into a steady, introspective groove during the verses. The emotional intensity swells in the choruses, shifting from internal monologue to a more expansive sense of wonder and release, before receding back into a hazy, contemplative state for the outro.
Application Scenarios & Imagery: Late-night drives through foggy coastal cities, underwater exploration sequences in film, or moments of solitary reflection in a dimly lit, modern living space.
Sonics & Production Profile: The mix features a wide, immersive soundstage with a distinct separation between the deep, centered low-end and the airy, spacious high frequencies. The production aesthetic is polished and atmospheric, utilizing heavy compression on the drums for punch while maintaining a breathable, reverberant quality on the melodic elements to enhance the "underwater" sensation.
Vocal Details
Vocal Gender & Timbre: A single male vocalist delivers the performance with a smooth, breathy tenor timbre that feels intimate and slightly distant.
Vocal Style: The delivery is characterized by a relaxed, almost whispered phrasing in the verses, conveying vulnerability. As the song moves into the chorus, the vocal intensity increases slightly with more sustained notes and layered harmonies, yet it retains a soft, ethereal quality throughout, avoiding aggressive belting.
Harmony/Backing Vocals: Self-layered harmonies appear prominently in the choruses and the post-chorus sections, creating a lush, choral texture that surrounds the lead line. These backing parts often utilize pitch-shifting and delay to enhance the dreamy atmosphere.
Vocal FX: The vocals are heavily processed with significant reverb and delay to create a sense of vast space. Subtle auto-tune is used as a stylistic effect rather than for correction, and occasional vocal chops and stutters are employed rhythmically in the post-chorus to act as percussive elements.
Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle Description (Primary/Secondary Layering):
Primary: A warm, detuned synthesizer pad serves as the harmonic bedrock, present from the very beginning and sustaining through the entire track to maintain the atmospheric mood.
Secondary: A deep, sub-bass synth enters shortly after the intro, locking in with the kick drum to provide weight. Brighter, plucked synth melodies appear intermittently to add counter-melodies, particularly during the pre-chorus and bridge sections.

Embellishments, Textures & Spatial FX: Atmospheric textures such as filtered white noise sweeps and reverse reverb tails are used extensively for transitions between sections.
