What is never collected

  • No names, emails, accounts or any personal data.
  • No IP addresses or precise location are stored to identify anyone.
  • No files, lyrics, prompts or anything you make.
  • You can turn the statistics off in any app at any moment — and nothing is sent.

Every app has a “What is sent” button that shows the whole report before it leaves your computer.
