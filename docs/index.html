What it does

A LoRA catalogue

131 LoRA picked from Hugging Face by likes and downloads, described and credited, and a search of everything Hugging Face has — every file checked for the model size it fits before it downloads.

A song from one line

The simple form: an idea in, a finished song out.

Your own LoRA

Songs of one artist or style become a LoRA on your card with HOT-Step's trainer and its LoKR recipe.

Stems and MIDI

Six stems with HT-Demucs; any track to multi-instrument MIDI with MuScriptor, with a piano roll played against the original.

A cover for every track

A track without one wears a free CC0 photograph from Wikimedia Commons that fits its style, a pattern in one of 21 styles, or a cover generated through OpenRouter.

Driven by your agent (MCP)

Connect Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Cursor and the agent does everything the studio does — 164 tools — and sees and works its window.

Questions

What graphics card do I need?

Windows 10/11 x64 and an NVIDIA GPU with 4 GB of VRAM or more (GTX 900 or newer). The models are downloaded from inside the studio when you decide to.

A runnable set is the DiT, the planner, the text encoder and the VAE, all in GGUF.

The studio preselects the set your card can run, but the download is always your decision. Every file is checked by SHA-256 against a pinned Hugging Face revision, and an interrupted download resumes.

Is it free?

Yes. The studio is free and open source, and the models download for free from inside it.

Does it work offline? Is my music sent anywhere?

Your music is yours, and it stays on your disk.

  • No account — There is nothing to sign up for.
  • No telemetry — The studio does not report what you generate.
  • Cloud is opt-in — Nothing leaves the machine until you add a key, and then only for that capability.
Can I use the songs I make?

The studio, acestep.cpp and ACE-Step 1.5 are MIT. The Regrind fine-tune is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0; each catalogue LoRA carries its author's licence.

Can I train it on my own style?

Songs of one artist or style become a LoRA on your card with HOT-Step's trainer and its LoKR recipe. Every setting is editable, and each checkpoint goes to the LoRA library in one click.

Can an AI agent use it?

Connect Claude Code, Claude Desktop or Cursor and the agent does everything the studio does — 164 tools — and sees and works its window. ACE-Step's writing rules come with it, so the agent writes the songs itself.

What is inside the download?

How is it built?

The service is compiled into the desktop binary and supervises the C++ engine. Finished songs are imported by the service itself, so a result survives a reload or a closed window.

Rust axum ┘        │
                   └─ acestep.cpp `ace-server`   (C++/GGML, GGUF)
